Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Sudoku Pal mobile app handles data.

Scope

This policy applies to data stored on your device and processing related to advertising services.

Data collected and processed

  • The app does not require an account; we do not collect direct personal information such as name or email address.
  • Game progress, settings, and similar data are stored locally on your device.
  • If ads are enabled, third-party ad providers such as Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, and ad impression/interaction data under their own privacy policies.

Use of data

  • Local data is used to maintain gameplay and your preferences.
  • Ad data is processed for ad delivery and performance measurement.

Data sharing

  • We do not sell or share personal data for marketing purposes.
  • Ad providers process data under their own policies: policies.google.com/privacy

Data deletion

You can delete local data and reset the app using the "Clear Data" option in the app settings.

Local data only

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is published with the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact details available on the platform where the app is published.
